The problem

Buyers shortlist with AI now

Most B2B buyers now use generative AI somewhere in their buying process, and AI answers shape which vendors make the shortlist.

Google rankings don't carry over

Fewer than 10% of the sources AI cites match the top 10 Google results. These systems read different signals.

AI names two or three tools

The answer is a shortlist, not a results page. You're in it or you're absent.

Straight answer

Is this worth doing yet?

Straight answer: AI search is a small share of web traffic today. Google is still far larger.

Traffic is the wrong measure. The shortlist forms before anyone clicks. A buyer who asks an assistant which tools to consider has narrowed the field to two or three names before your site ever sees a visit, and that happens whether or not the click ever lands on you.

What's changed is the direction. The channel is growing quickly, the traffic converts better in B2B, and almost nobody in your category has done the work.

The technical fixes are inexpensive and they last. Citation presence compounds, which means it's cheaper to build now than in two years when your competitors are entrenched. The real question is whether you'd rather be positioned before this matters or after.

If the honest answer for you is “later,” that's a reasonable call, and we'd rather say it than sell past it.

FAQ

Questions

Isn't this just SEO?
Partly, and we'll say so. For Google's AI Overviews, Google states it's still SEO, and they're right. ChatGPT accounts for the large majority of AI referral traffic and doesn't run on Google's index, so that part is a separate problem with separate inputs.
Couldn't we do this in house?
Some teams do. It needs someone who can run the question set across six engines consistently, read the results against competitor movement, work the citation sources your category is built from, and keep the technical layer current as the site changes. That's a role, not a task. If you have someone free to own it, you don't need us.
How long until we see movement?
Technical fixes take effect in days to weeks. Citation work takes one to three months. We'll tell you at audit stage what's realistic for your situation.
Do you need access to our site?
No. We work from public data.
What if the audit says we're already doing well?
Then we'll tell you, and you'll know. It happens, and it's worth the $750 to find out.
